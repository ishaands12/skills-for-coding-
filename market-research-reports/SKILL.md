# Market Research Reports Skill Overview

This comprehensive skill generates professional 50+ page market research reports modeled after top consulting firms like McKinsey and BCG. Here are the key aspects:

## Core Features

The skill delivers "professional-grade reports of 50+ pages with extensive visual content" using LaTeX formatting and professional styling. It integrates multiple strategic frameworks including Porter's Five Forces, PESTLE analysis, SWOT, and TAM/SAM/SOM market sizing.

## Visual Requirements

Reports must include 5-6 essential visuals generated at the start, with additional graphics added during writing. The skill leverages two primary tools:

- **scientific-schematics**: For charts, diagrams, Porter's Five Forces, competitive matrices, and strategic frameworks
- **generate-image**: For executive infographics and conceptual illustrations

## Report Structure

The standard document contains:
- Front matter (~5 pages)
- 11 core analytical chapters (~35 pages) covering market overview, sizing, trends, competition, customers, technology, regulations, and risks
- Strategic recommendations and implementation roadmaps (~10 pages)
- Back matter with methodology and appendices (~5 pages)

## Data Integration

The skill "deep integration with research-lookup for data gathering" enables comprehensive market research through structured queries about market sizing, competitive landscapes, industry trends, and regulatory environments.

## Quality Standards

Reports must exceed 50 pages total, with minimum page targets specified for each section. All statistics require sources, projections must state assumptions, and visuals must render at 300+ DPI with consistent professional styling.
